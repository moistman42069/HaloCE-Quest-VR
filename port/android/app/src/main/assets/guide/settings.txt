NATIVE SETTINGS REFERENCE

Defaults apply to new settings. Existing saved values win except the one-time VR Safe geometry migration (revision 1); subsequent explicit Safe/Normal choices are preserved. Test18 also applies third-person/right-controller vehicle defaults once; later vehicle selections persist. VR settings apply to Quest only; desktop-only options do not affect Android. Restart for renderer, networking and other startup options. Use the in-game VR pages for live supported adjustments. The integrated project updater checks automatically in the launcher; Versions & updates controls checks and installation; inherited update.auto does not replace the mod with upstream.

display.fullscreen [boolean]
Default: true
Start fullscreen, drawing at the display's resolution and shape; false
starts in a window, which draws the Xbox's 640x480. F11 switches.

display.window_scale [integer]
Default: 2
The window's size as a multiple of 640x480 (it can be resized).

Default: 0
Columns of the 480-line picture: 0 for the display's shape, 640 for the

display.vsync [boolean]
Default: true
Wait for the display between frames; false draws as fast as possible.

display.interpolation [boolean]
Default: true
Draw a frame for every display refresh, blending between the game's 30
ticks a second; false keeps the original 30 frames a second.

display.direct_camera [boolean]
Default: true
In first person, point the view where the player aims now instead of
where the last tick left it: the view turns the frame the mouse moves,
not up to two ticks (66 ms) later.

display.high_res_hud [boolean]
Default: true
Draw the HUD (meters, counters, panels, motion sensor, reticles,
waypoints, scopes) from the high-res assets (8x the maps' bitmaps);
false draws the maps' own bitmaps.

display.high_res_text [boolean]
Default: true
Draw the menus' and HUD's text with the fonts in port/assets/fonts
(Overpass) at the display's resolution, and the menus' titles from
port/assets/titles; false draws the maps' bitmap fonts and titles.

audio.enabled [boolean]
Default: true
Play sound.

The volume of everything, 0.0 to 1.0.

Default: 1.0
How far the view turns for the mouse's movement.

Default: false

Default: false

Default: "important"

The language the game asks the Xbox for: "ja", "de", "fr", "es" or "it";

Default on in VR, off in flat Android. Can reduce performance. Not a data revision selector.

vr.arms [string]
Default: "ik"
With vr.aim "hand": the first-person arms. "ik" reaches them from the
shoulders to the hands (the left to the left controller, or to the gun
when held near it); "hidden" shows the gun alone; "animated" moves

vr.two_handed [string]
Default: "auto"
With vr.aim "hand": holding the gun in both hands steadies it, pointing
from the weapon hand to the other. "auto" (default): the other hand resting at the
gun's support grip locks there, until pulled away; "grip": it locks only while its
grip is squeezed there; "off": no attachment.

vr.two_hand_auto_applied [boolean]
Default: false
Internal one-time migration (test21): a config on the old "grip" default moves to

vr.left_handed [boolean]
Default: false
Left-handed play: the gun starts in the left hand (fire, grenade and zoom swap triggers
and bumpers) and, with vr.mirror_controls "auto", the sticks and face buttons mirror.
Changing it in the menu also mirrors vehicle steering and Move With when they used a
hand. Palms together and the other hand's grip still pass the gun across.

vr.mirror_controls [string]
Default: "auto"
"auto": with vr.left_handed, move on the right stick and turn on the left, and the
face buttons swap hands (jump and reload on the left controller). "off": the standard
layout (move on the left stick) whichever hand holds the gun.

Default: false
Internal one-time migration (test20d): a config already left-handed keeps standard

vr.weapons [string]
Default: "physical"
With vr.aim "hand": "locked" keeps the gun in the weapon hand always;
"physical" keeps it there while that hand's grip holds it (the trigger
fires whatever gun is in the hand): let go, it falls - at a holster it
is put away, the hand empty, and with the other hand on it, it passes to
that hand. A gun the game puts in the hand (a level's start, a pickup)
stays supported until the first grip. An empty hand gripping at a
holster draws the weapon there, and by a weapon lying about takes it.
Grenades then go on the left's lower button (a tap throws, a hold
switches). In network games only with vr.physical_multiplayer.

vr.physical_multiplayer [boolean]
Default: false
Physical weapons (vr.weapons) in network games too, where the other
machines may not see the guns dropped and taken; false keeps network
games to the locked behaviour.

vr.holsters [boolean]

coming into one): its grip there switches weapons, or with physical
weapons (vr.weapons) a gun let go there is put away and an empty hand
gripping there draws one.

vr.hand_tracking [string]
Default: "ik"
How tracked hands follow the controllers (with vr.arms "ik"): "ik" body-IK arms reach
from the shoulders; "floating" hands go exactly where the controllers are, with no
arms; "floating_arms" hands go exactly to the controllers and arms hang from a
shoulder that follows them.

vr.calibration_split_applied [boolean]
Default: false
Internal one-time migration (test20c): hand-comfort rotations saved in vr.align_* move to
vr.hand_*, so the gun returns to the controller's aim. Roll flips (+-180) stay.

vr.gun_anchor [boolean]
Default: true
Anchors the held gun to the controller: the gun hand's wrist sits where the empty hand's
wrist would, for every weapon, and the gun turns about the hand. false restores the
older camera placement (each weapon's animation decides where the gun sits).

vr.gun_forward [real]
Default: 0.0
With vr.gun_anchor: moves the held gun along its barrel, in metres (-0.2..0.2;
negative pulls it back toward you).

vr.gun_up [real]
Default: 0.0
As vr.gun_forward, up (negative is down).

vr.gun_out [real]
Default: 0.0
As vr.gun_forward, away from your body's middle (right for the right hand, left for
the left hand; negative is inward).

vr.weapon_offset_right [real]
Default: 0.10
Advanced. How far right of the weapon camera the gun hand's grip sits, in metres
(placing that camera). With vr.gun_anchor the gun stays in the hand whatever this is; it
moves only the point the wall check measures from. Adjust the gun with vr.gun_* instead.

vr.weapon_offset_up [real]
Default: -0.12
Advanced; as vr.weapon_offset_right, above the camera (negative is below).

vr.weapon_offset_back [real]
Default: -0.20
Advanced; as vr.weapon_offset_right, behind the camera (negative is ahead).

vr.snap_turn [real]
Default: 0.0
Degrees the right stick turns you at a flick; 0 turns smoothly instead.

vr.smooth_turn_speed [real]
Default: 120.0
With vr.snap_turn 0: degrees a second the right stick turns you at full
push.

graphics.preset [string]
Default: "auto"
Which effects the headset draws (port/linux/game/vr_graphics.c): "low",
"medium", "high", or "max" (high, and with vr.resolution_scale 0 a
sharper picture than the headset recommends). "auto": low on the first
Quest, medium on the Quest 2, high otherwise. The settings below override
it one effect at a time.
